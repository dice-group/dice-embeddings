# ULTRA

`dicee/models/ultra.py` adapts the architecture and graph construction of
[DeepGraphLearning/ULTRA](https://github.com/DeepGraphLearning/ULTRA), commit
`427966ad8ed60420eef034063d44f3153addff90`. Reference fixtures were generated
using that implementation. The reusable grouped sampling and adversarial loss
follow its training recipe.
